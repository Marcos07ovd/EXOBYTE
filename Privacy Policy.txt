Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 30/03/2025

EXOBYTE values your privacy and is committed to protecting the data we collect. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect:

- User, server, and role IDs: For configuring and personalizing the bot's functionalities.
- Server join/leave logs: We monitor when the bot is added or removed from servers.
- Applications (Staff, Dev, Betatester) and partnerships: Data voluntarily provided via Discord forms.
- Suggestions: Collected through the /sugerencias_exobyte command.

How We Use This Information:

- To configure and customize the bot on servers.
- To manage applications and partnerships.
- To improve user experience based on suggestions.
- For security and adoption analytics (server join/leave tracking).

Data Protection:

- Data is securely stored and not shared with third parties.
- We implement technical measures to prevent unauthorized access.
